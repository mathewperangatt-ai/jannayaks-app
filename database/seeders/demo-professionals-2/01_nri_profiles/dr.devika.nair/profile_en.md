Her early interest in medicine developed gradually, shaped by the practical example of relatives who worked in education and healthcare. She became particularly interested in helping patients understand the purpose of routine monitoring and the practical steps that make treatment plans easier to follow. Devika has also taken part in small health-awareness sessions organised for community groups. These sessions focus on general health education and encourage participants to seek appropriate professional advice rather than rely on informal remedies or incomplete information. She prefers interactive discussions in which people can raise ordinary concerns without feeling embarrassed.

Their experiences have reinforced her belief that accessible healthcare information and clear communication matter across age groups and backgrounds.

Outside work, she enjoys reading Malayalam fiction, walking, and exploring the city's cultural and food communities with friends. She makes periodic visits to Kerala to spend time with family. These visits help her remain connected to the language, places and everyday social relationships that shaped her early life.

She hopes to continue developing this patient-centred approach while contributing to the wider professional community.
